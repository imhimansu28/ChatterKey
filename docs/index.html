Before you start.

Does voice typing need internet? Where does my audio go?

Yes. Audio and instructions go directly to your selected provider; voice edits also send the selected text. Provider data policies apply. Ordinary Android keyboard typing works offline. Read the privacy details.

Can I dictate in Hindi or Hinglish?

Use preserve-language instructions or Translate to English mode. Quality depends on the audio model and recording. Android’s typing layout is English (India); a dedicated Hindi layout and explicit Roman/Devanagari controls aren’t included yet.

Will voice editing work in every app?

Not every editor exposes a verifiable selection. Review the proposed edit; use Copy and manual paste if replacement isn’t available. Voice processing never sends your message automatically. See the Mac or Android editing guide.
